Create a five-part isometric pixel-art animation on a clean white background. Show a small farm changing through the seasons, a town being built, a harbour moving from day into night, a volcanic island erupting and a rocket lifting off. Keep each miniature scene on a square diorama with a consistent camera angle and pixel scale. Use subtle ambient movement, readable chapter titles and a small progress indicator.
