```text
Create a stylized 2D side-view action short set in an industrial compound at sunset. Use a compact grey protagonist with a distinctive red visor and clearly separated silhouettes for the opponents. Plan a readable sequence of movement, dodges and close combat, with anticipation before each action and a brief hold after each impact. Add camera shakes, muzzle flashes, debris and matching sound effects without obscuring the choreography. Finish with a bold title card and export the complete video.
```
