## Recreate in PagePop

```text
Build an explorable voxel village tucked between enormous mountains. Arrange small houses, paths, trees and gathering spaces so the settlement feels lived in. Add simple villagers with a few everyday actions, atmospheric lighting and gentle environmental movement. Keep a consistent voxel scale and make the village readable from both a wide camera and ground level. Provide clear movement and camera controls, then deliver the interactive browser scene and a short guided video tour.
```
